— HOW WE WORK

A complete engagement.

We work alongside your team to design, build, and deploy. Every engagement follows a four-step process refined to ship production-grade AI in weeks, not quarters.

01

Discover

We audit your workflow and data to find the highest-leverage place for AI. You leave week one with a scoped problem and a build plan.

02

Design

We design the system around your constraints — compliance, data, existing tools. You approve the design before we write a line of code.

03

Build

2-week sprints with working demos at every checkpoint. Models, agents, evals, infrastructure — all owned end-to-end.

04

Deploy & Govern

We deploy, set up monitoring, and stay on for 90 days to make sure it works in the real world.

— SOLUTIONS WE BUILD

Real problems. Production AI. Measurable outcomes.

An example of the kind of system we design — drawn from a real problem we've scoped and are ready to build for the right partner.

RECRUITMENT / TALENT ACQUISITION

Resume Scoring Agent

The Challenge

The bottleneck wasn't volume — it was attention. By resume forty, the standard had drifted. By resume eighty, equally strong candidates were getting different verdicts depending on who reviewed them and when.

The System

A scoring agent that reads each resume against the job description, ranks candidates, and surfaces a shortlist with written rationale — so recruiters do the judging, not the reading.
