The Challenge

Recruitment agencies spend 3–4 hours per open role on resume screening — downloading resumes, opening PDFs, scanning for skills, comparing candidates manually, updating spreadsheets, shortlisting. The work is repetitive, inconsistent, and cognitively draining, and keeps your senior recruiters buried in resume filtering instead of closing candidates and clients.

At scale, this bottleneck compounds. A 1,000-resume inflow per week means 200–300 hours of wasted work — work that doesn't require human judgment, just attention and time.

The System
An end-to-end candidate intelligence agent

The agent is triggered by inbound candidate flow — email, ATS, Google Forms — and immediately begins ingesting resumes and profile data from multiple sources. It parses each resume into a structured candidate profile.

The agent scores and ranks candidates against job descriptions using semantic matching, skill extraction, and experience mapping. It cross-references candidates across sources, identifying best-fit profiles and edge cases. Missing data, inconsistencies, or low-confidence matches are flagged with context and confidence scores — not hidden.

The output is a ranked candidate dashboard with a natural language interface. The recruiter opens the dashboard, asks "show top backend engineers with 3–5 years experience," reviews the shortlist, takes action, and moves on. Total recruiter time: 30–40 minutes per role.

Deployment

The system integrates with the client's ATS, email inbox, and form pipelines via API and webhook. No change to existing candidate sourcing workflows — the agent runs automatically on inbound data from ingestion to ranked output. Recruiters receive notifications with top-ranked candidates directly in their dashboard or inbox.

Monitoring tracks match accuracy, recruiter usage, and shortlist quality. Feedback loop: recruiters refine rankings and signal corrections, improving matching accuracy over time.
