— HOW WE WORK

A complete engagement.

We work alongside your team to design, build, and deploy. Every engagement follows a four-step process refined to ship production-grade AI in weeks, not quarters.

01

Discover

We audit your workflow and data to find the highest-leverage place for AI. You leave week one with a scoped problem and a build plan.

— SOLUTIONS WE BUILD

Real problems. Production AI. Measurable outcomes.

An example of the kind of system we design — drawn from a real problem we've scoped and are ready to build for the right partner.

RECRUITMENT / TALENT ACQUISITION

Candidate Intelligence Agent

The Challenge

Recruitment agencies spend 3–4 hours per open role on resume screening — downloading, scanning, comparing, shortlisting manually. At 1,000 resumes a week, that's 200–300 hours of work that doesn't require human judgment, just time.

The System

An agent that ingests resumes from email, ATS, and forms — parses, scores, and ranks candidates against job descriptions — and surfaces a shortlist through a natural language dashboard recruiters can query in plain English.

70% reduction in screening time
2x faster shortlisting
